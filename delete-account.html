Delete your account

SkoolBuddy for Parents

Last updated: June 25, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your SkoolBuddy for Parents account and the data associated with it.

What gets deleted

  • Your account record (name, email, profile picture).
  • All deadlines, notes, and reminders you created.
  • Child entries and category data you added.
  • Push notification tokens linked to your account.

How to request deletion

Send an email to dues.assistant@gmail.com from the Google account you use to sign in to SkoolBuddy, with the subject line:

Subject: Delete my SkoolBuddy account

We will confirm your request and delete your account and associated data within 30 days.

Partial data deletion

You can also delete individual deadlines, notes, or children directly inside the app at any time, without deleting your account.

If you share a family workspace

SkoolBuddy lets two parents share deadlines for the same children. When you delete your account, the entries you personally created are removed. Entries created by the second parent remain in their account.

Questions

Email dues.assistant@gmail.com if you have any questions about this process.
